Dynamic Capsule

Privacy Policy

Plain-English privacy terms for the Dynamic Capsule app.

Last updated: August 19, 2026

Our Core Promise

Dynamic Capsule is designed to run 100% locally on your phone. We do not have servers, we do not track your activity, and we never collect, store, sell, or share your personal data.

When you use Dynamic Capsule, all information processed to create the interactive capsule interface (like notifications, music metadata, or incoming call alerts) is read, transformed, and displayed immediately on your screen. None of this data is saved to a file, and none of it is sent over the internet.

How We Use Permissions

To recreate the notch utility experience, Android requires the app to ask for specific permissions. Here is a simple explanation of what they do and why they are necessary:

Show Over Other Apps
Allows the capsule to sit at the top of your screen, floating above whatever app you are currently using, so you can always see and interact with it.
Notification Access
Allows the app to read notifications as they arrive, so we can temporarily format and show them to you inside the capsule. The content is never saved or sent anywhere.
Read Phone State
Allows the app to detect incoming phone calls so it can display the caller's name and give you quick action buttons (answer/decline) directly in the capsule.
Bluetooth Connect
Allows the capsule to show a quick status message when you connect accessories like Bluetooth earbuds or smartwatches.
Run in Background
Needed so the Android system does not kill the capsule utility service when you close the main settings screen or put your phone in standby.
Start on Startup
Automatically starts the capsule service once your phone boots up, so you don't have to manually launch the app every time you restart.

Data Storage & Security

Since we do not collect, transmit, or save any data, there is no personal data stored on servers. The app works strictly on your phone, meaning your privacy is fully protected under Android's built-in operating system security barriers.

Children's Privacy

Our app is safe for everyone. We do not gather, store, or share information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Parents can rest easy knowing that no information is being harvested or communicated back to us or any advertisers.

Changes to this Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. When we make updates, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to check this page periodically for any changes.
